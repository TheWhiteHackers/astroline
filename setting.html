Change Settings

These settings make Astroline easier to see and read. Click a button to turn it ON or OFF. Your choices are saved and work on every page.

Makes all the words on every page about a quarter bigger, so they're easier to read.

Swaps red, green and pink for blue and orange, which most people with color blindness can tell apart. The checklist also says OK or NEED, so you never have to go by color alone.

Changes the text to OpenDyslexic, a font with heavier bottoms on the letters so they're less likely to look flipped or jumbled. Some people with dyslexia find it easier to read. (Needs internet to load.)

Rocket animations turn off by themselves if your computer is set to reduce motion.
